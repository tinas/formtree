Initial release. A form is a tree of definitions, and every node of it, from a wizard step to a single field, validates and submits on its own. `@formtree/core` is the framework-free engine with Standard Schema validation, conditional branches, dependencies and asynchronous checks. `@formtree/vue` and `@formtree/react` bind it to components, and `@formtree/vue` shows forms in Vue Devtools.
